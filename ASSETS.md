# Website assets

- `fonts/bricolage-grotesque.woff2` is Bricolage Grotesque, sourced from Google
  Fonts. Its SIL Open Font License is in `fonts/OFL-Bricolage-Grotesque.txt`.
- `/tiles.js` is the LicoLand cipher alphabet and is owned by this repository.
  The product sites carry unchanged copies.
- `/cipher.js` draws the page as ciphertext and is owned by this repository.
- `/favicon.svg` is the organization star on a night tile.

All served assets are local to this site.
